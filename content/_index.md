I build software that uses AI, and I explain how it works to the people who have to make decisions about it.

I'm Charles Max Wood. I have spent two decades writing software, and a good chunk of that interviewing the people who write it, which turns out to be decent training for explaining hard technical things to people who do not do this for a living. Building and explaining are the same job to me, and this is where both of them live.
